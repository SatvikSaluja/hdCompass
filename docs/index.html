Head direction without behavioural labels

The pipeline finds a ring-shaped representation of neural activity, estimates tuning from that ring, and uses a hidden Markov model (HMM) to decode the internal compass. Tracked head angle is used for validation and for the supervised reference decoder.

Positive finding: the label-free HMM reaches 0.406 rad held-out error in Mouse32 and a median 0.36 rad across 22 post-subiculum sessions. SWS diffusion exceeds wake in 21 of those 22 sessions.
Boundary: ring detection is less reliable than decoding, real-data posteriors are overconfident, and the REM–wake difference does not replicate across datasets.

The density filter was explored after the initial Mouse32 failure, then adopted through checks with fixed decision rules before analysing the second dataset. Its success is not universal; the short 120-second smoke slice also misses the ring with the filter.

Ground-truth simulations

Two 17,000-second synthetic sessions put label-free decoding within 0.012 rad of a true-model decoder in every state. Wake and REM σ are recovered to the nearest grid point. Synthetic wake/REM 90% posterior sets cover roughly 90–92% of true angles; coverage falls to about 82% in SWS with jumps.

Uncertainty: on held-out real wake with tracked-angle tuning, nominal 90% posterior sets cover only 64% of true angles. Use posterior variance as a relative measure across states, not an absolute error bar.
Remaining limits
  • ADn evidence comes from one animal and one session; the 22-session replication is in a different brain region.
  • σ is fitted on a coarse grid with a factor of 1.76 between adjacent values.
  • Jump probability is fixed at zero. Decoded jump counts underestimate synthetic SWS jumps by about 20×.
  • Sleep decoding uses wake tuning; non-uniform changes in firing rates or tuning width remain untested.

Reading the scores

Circular MAE
Mean shortest angular distance between estimated and tracked heading. Lower is better; 0.406 rad is about 23°.
σ
Fitted diffusion scale of the latent compass, in radians per square root second. Higher values indicate faster modelled diffusion.
Circular variance
Spread of the decoded angular posterior. Larger values indicate less concentrated estimates.
Ring score
Ratio of the longest to the second-longest persistent H1 lifetime, assessed against shuffled data.
REM / SWS
Rapid-eye-movement sleep / slow-wave sleep.
